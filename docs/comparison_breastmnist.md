# Selector comparison on BreastMNIST: first complete run

NEXT.md step 3. Run 2026-10-31 with `configs/comparison_breastmnist.yaml`:
the same design as `docs/comparison_scanb.md` on 780 breast ultrasound
images as 2,048 ResNet-50 embedding dimensions, one task: malignant (210)
against benign or normal (570). 5 splits at train fraction 0.5, 85
executions, 11 seconds on 6 workers.

Tables generated by:

    python scripts/summarize_comparison.py results/comparison_breastmnist.csv \
        --n-features 2048 --out <file>

Each cell is the median over the 5 splits, with the range in brackets.

## What it shows

- Every selector beats the random control at every k. At k = 10 the gain is
  0.16 (RFE) to 0.28 (Lasso) macro-F1; at k = 250 it is 0.06 to 0.07.
- Lasso and tree importance lead at k = 10 (0.71 and 0.72). Lasso leads at
  k = 250 (0.82), with only 162 to 182 non-zero weights.
- RFE is weakest at k = 10 (0.63) but catches up by k = 250 (0.80). Unlike
  on SCAN-B, it beats random at every k here.
- HC2008 keeps 204 dimensions, again the edge of its search (alpha0 = 0.1
  of 2,048), and scores 0.778. HC2004 keeps 1 or 2 and scores 0.522.
- Mann-Whitney and HC2008 are again the most stable (Kuncheva about 0.66).

## Caveats

- One task and five splits: the ranges are wide, and most differences
  between the four non-random selectors at k = 50 and 250 lie within them.
- RFE uses step 0.5, as on SCAN-B.
