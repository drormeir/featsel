# Selector comparison on BreastMNIST

NEXT.md steps 3 and 6. Run 2026-10-31 with
`configs/comparison_breastmnist.yaml`: the same design as
`docs/comparison_scanb.md` on 780 breast ultrasound images as 2,048
ResNet-50 embedding dimensions, one task: malignant (210) against benign or
normal (570). 20 splits at train fraction 0.5, k = 5 to 250, 680
executions. The first 5 splits at k = 10, 50, 204 and 250 come from the
first run; `--resume` added the rest, 585 executions in 43 seconds on 6
workers.

Tables generated by:

    python scripts/summarize_comparison.py results/comparison_breastmnist.csv \
        --n-features 2048 --out <file>

Each cell is the median over the 20 splits, with the first and third
quartiles in brackets.

## What it shows

- In the median, every selector beats the random control at every k. The
  gain is about 0.2 macro-F1 from k = 5 to 20, falls from k = 50 on, and is
  0.05 to 0.07 at k = 250.
- Lasso has the highest median at every k. Its quartiles overlap tree
  importance's at every k, so the order of the two is not settled.
- RFE is weakest up to k = 20 and catches up from k = 100.
- HC2008 keeps 204 dimensions, the edge of its search (alpha0 = 0.1 of
  2,048), and equals Mann-Whitney at k = 204 on every split. It gains 0.060
  [0.037, 0.084] over random selection of 204. With 5 splits that gain's
  range included zero; with 20 its quartiles do not. HC2004 keeps 1 to 6
  and scores 0.521.
- Mann-Whitney and HC2008 are the most stable (Kuncheva about 0.65). Lasso
  is the least (0.14 to 0.21).

## Caveats

- One task: every number rests on the same 780 images.
- RFE uses step 0.5, as on SCAN-B.
