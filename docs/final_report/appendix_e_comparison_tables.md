# Appendix E. Full tables of the comparison

[Back to contents](README.md)

The full output of the comparison of chapter 7, run 2026-10-31. Each cell
is the median over the splits (16 on SCAN-B, 20 on BreastMNIST), with the
first and third quartiles in brackets. Tables generated by
`scripts/summarize_comparison.py` from
[`results/comparison_scanb.csv`](../../results/comparison_scanb.csv) and
[`results/comparison_breastmnist.csv`](../../results/comparison_breastmnist.csv),
with `--n-features 28552` for SCAN-B and `--n-features 2048` for
BreastMNIST.

The selection times here were taken before the GPU work of chapter 6, on
the CPU, with 4 workers sharing the machine. Chapter 8 has the current
times.
